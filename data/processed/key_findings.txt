KEY NON-OBVIOUS FINDINGS FROM RRF PURPOSE ANALYSIS
============================================================

TOP INSIGHTS (Surprising Patterns Only):
----------------------------------------

1. Urban bars are 3.3x as likely to select Rent

2. bakeries are 0.5x less likely to select Maintenance Indoor compared to similar businesses
   - Rate: 67.4% vs baseline 79.3%

3. bakeries are 0.6x less likely to select Covered Supplier compared to similar businesses
   - Rate: 48.9% vs baseline 62.5%

4. bakeries are 0.6x less likely to select Food compared to similar businesses
   - Rate: 77.8% vs baseline 86.1%

5. bakeries are 0.6x less likely to select Debt compared to similar businesses
   - Rate: 32.9% vs baseline 43.7%

6. bakeries are 0.5x less likely to select Payroll compared to similar businesses
   - Rate: 91.7% vs baseline 95.9%

7. brewpubs are 0.6x less likely to select Maintenance Indoor compared to similar businesses
   - Rate: 71.7% vs baseline 81.2%

8. Urban producers are 3.5x as likely to select Rent

9. brewpubs are 1.5x more likely to select Outdoor Seating Construction compared to similar businesses
   - Rate: 56.9% vs baseline 46.2%

10. brewpubs are 0.6x less likely to select Supplies compared to similar businesses
   - Rate: 77.8% vs baseline 84.5%


METHODOLOGY NOTE:
----------------------------------------
All findings shown are statistically significant (p < 0.01) and
represent departures from structural expectations. Obvious patterns
(e.g., food trucks not paying rent) have been filtered out.
